Key Architectural Differentiators

🔄 Unmet-Demand Loop

Missed searches are logged into SQLite -> Azure OpenAI generates stocking advice for shopkeepers -> automatically notifies shoppers the moment goods arrive.

🛵 Shared Delivery Service

Haversine formula automatically groups shops within a 1 km radius into a cluster. All shops share ONE rider (₹12,000 / N shops = low cost per shopkeeper).

🏷️ FEFO Batch Inventory

Stock is tracked in batches with hand-entered expiries. At checkout, nearest-expiry batches sell first. Near-expiry batches power "Use Soon" discount deals.
